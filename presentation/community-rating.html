Context

User Problem

As HelloFresh has expanded menu offerings (20+ recipes weekly in some markets), users face increasing decision fatigue during meal selection. Without trusted signals or recommendations, users may:

  • Spend excessive time evaluating options
  • Experience choice paralysis
  • Feel uncertain about their selections
  • Default to familiar choices, limiting variety

Proposed Solution

Display community ratings prominently throughout the meal selection journey as social proof, providing users with reliable signals to make confident decisions faster.

Key touchpoints: Recipe cards (browse view), recipe detail pages

Success Metrics

Selection Confidence (Primary)
  • Time to complete meal selection
  • Menu completion rate (% users who finalize box)
  • Meal variety score (diversity of selections)
  • Recipe trial rate (% users trying new vs repeat)
Business Impact (Secondary)
  • AOV (Average Order Value)
  • AOR (Add-On Rate)
  • Retention rate (4-week, 8-week)
  • Premium recipe selection rate

Rating Engagement Bet

By explicitly showcasing how ratings help the community make better choices, users will be motivated to contribute. Displaying users' own past ratings creates personal value and reinforces rating behavior.

Engagement Metrics
  • Rating submission rate (% delivered meals rated)
  • Review addition rate (ratings with text)
  • Average rating speed (days post-delivery)
  • Repeat rater rate (% users rating multiple weeks)

Questions to Discuss

▶
Strategic Priority

Should we prioritize volume (many ratings, few reviews) or depth (detailed reviews)? Quality reviews are more helpful for decision-making but harder to obtain.

Popularity Bias

Will prominent ratings create a rich-get-richer effect where high-rated meals dominate selections? Is this acceptable, or do we need mechanisms to promote variety?

Moderation Strategy

What's our approach to negative reviews, fake reviews, and content moderation? Resource implications?

Timing Strategy

When do we prompt for ratings? Immediately post-cooking vs. next-day vs. pre-next-selection? What drives highest quality and quantity?

Integration with Personalization

How do community ratings interact with our ML recommendation engine? Do they complement or compete?

Customer Support

Expected volume increase in rating-related inquiries? Support team training needed?

Feature Roadmap: Milestone Evolution

How community rating features evolve from MVP to full scope experience

Milestone 1

Foundation: Establish Trust

Problem

Users face decision paralysis with 20+ weekly recipes and lack trusted signals for confident meal selection.

Goal

Establish ratings as a reliable decision-making tool and create baseline rating behavior.

Track

Rating submission rate, time to menu completion, recipe trial rate (new vs repeat)

Signal

Success: Increased rating rate, faster menu completion. Risk: Low engagement, negative feedback dominance

Features

  • PDP top rating display
  • Overall rating section
  • Rating prompt banner
  • Community reviews (subset defined by cookbook)
  • User review card
  • Old rating drawer
Milestone 2

Insights: Deepen Understanding

Builds on M1

Problem

Users need more context beyond star ratings to understand why recipes are highly rated and what makes them work.

Goal

Provide deeper insights through distribution data, AI summaries, and thematic analysis to drive informed choices.

Track

AI summary engagement, theme click-through, rating distribution interaction, menu browse patterns

Signal

Success: Increased variety in selections, higher recipe trial rate. Risk: Information overload, AI summary trust issues

New Features

  • Rating distribution
  • AI summary CTA
  • Comment themes
  • Menu card ratings
  • Community reviews (new logic for subset)
Milestone 3

Rich Content: Personalize Discovery

Builds on M1 + M2

Problem

Users want to find recipes matching specific preferences (taste, difficulty, dietary) and see visual proof before selection.

Goal

Enable self-service discovery through filtering, visual content, and improved rating UX to match personal preferences.

Track

Filter usage, photo review engagement, rating completion rate (new drawer), menu filter adoption, theme filter patterns

Signal

Success: Higher satisfaction scores, reduced skip rate. Risk: Filter bias creates echo chamber, photo requirements slow submissions

New Features

  • Filters (rating & themes)
  • Theme overview
  • Use photo captured from cookbook flow
  • New rating drawer
  • Menu filters
Later

Future: Community Engagement

Data-driven enhancements

Problem

To be determined based on M1-M3 performance data and observed user engagement patterns.

Goal

Enhance community quality and reduce noise through moderation tools and engagement mechanics.

Track

Review quality metrics, spam/fake review reports, helpful button usage, community moderation effectiveness

Signal

Success: Improved review quality perception, reduced support tickets. Risk: Over-moderation dampens honest feedback

Potential Features

  • Helpful/Report buttons
  • Photo from feedback
  • Engagement features based on user behavior
