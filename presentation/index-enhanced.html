Overview

The Opportunity

Recipe ratings are valuable but underutilized. This initiative creates a visible, engaging rating system that motivates users to share feedback and helps others make confident meal choices.

Problem
Currently, recipe ratings are not prominently displayed throughout the platform. Users don't see the value their ratings provide to the community, reducing motivation to participate.
Hypothesis
If we display community ratings prominently and provide contextual rating opportunities, users will be motivated to rate recipes because they see their contributions influence the community.
Success Metric
Achieve increased rating rate through visible community ratings and strategic rating entry points across cooking flow, past orders, and recipe details.
"Make ratings visible everywhere, provide feedback opportunities at contextually relevant moments, and create a feedback loop where contributions directly benefit the community."
